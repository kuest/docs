<Info>
The Dashboard opens at <code>/admin</code>. Platform settings remain available under <strong>Admin → General</strong> at <code>/admin/general</code>.
</Info>

Use the Dashboard as the daily operating view: resolve time-sensitive market issues first, then review growth, confirmed trading activity, and fee history.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Event attention" icon="volleyball" href="/admin/events">
    Find active sports events missing a provider ID, past-due markets awaiting resolution, and user resolution reports awaiting review.
  </Card>
  <Card title="User growth" icon="users" href="/admin/users">
    See total registered users and daily registrations in a compact interactive chart.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trading volume" icon="chart-no-axes-combined" href="/admin/events">
    Follow confirmed trading volume attributed to your site, with a daily volume history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fee history" icon="hand-coins" href="/admin/affiliate">
    Review total operator and affiliate earnings, with daily history.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Recommended operating order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear event alerts">
    Review missing sports provider IDs, past-due markets, and resolution reports before they affect score sync or settlement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check platform activity">
    Compare recent registrations and confirmed trading volume with the previous days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review fees">
    Review fee history, then open [Affiliate & Fees](/admin/affiliate) to check the current claimable balance and withdraw when needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the cards show

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sports events missing an ID" icon="volleyball" defaultOpen>
    Counts visible, active sports and esports events that are not connected to TheSportsDB or PandaScore. Open the card to go to Events with the matching attention filter already applied.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Markets awaiting resolution" icon="gavel">
    Counts visible, active events whose end date has passed while at least one active market remains unresolved. Open the card to inspect the matching events directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Resolution reports" icon="message-square-warning">
    Counts user proposals associated with active unresolved markets from allowed creators. Open the card to find events with reports and review the proposed outcomes before resolving them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Registered users" icon="users">
    Shows the total number of registered users and the number added in the last seven days. The chart plots daily registrations over the last 30 days. Hover over it to inspect the date and value.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Trading volume" icon="chart-no-axes-combined">
    Shows all-time confirmed trading volume attributed to your site and the last 30 days of daily volume. Unfilled orders do not contribute.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fee history" icon="hand-coins">
    Shows cumulative operator and affiliate earnings, with daily history over the last 30 days. This is historical income; the currently withdrawable balance is shown in [Affiliate & Fees](/admin/affiliate).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
Missing sports ID and past-due event alerts exclude hidden or inactive events. If a metric cannot be loaded, the Dashboard shows a dash rather than a zero.
</Note>
