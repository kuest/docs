## Site domain

Attach the public site domain, such as `markets.example.com`, to the hosting provider running your frontend. In the guided launch, you can add and verify it in the result panel; for manual deployments, use your provider's domain settings and the DNS records it supplies.

After DNS verification and HTTPS are ready:

1. Set `SITE_URL` to the canonical public HTTPS URL and redeploy the frontend.
2. Add that URL to the Reown project's **Configuration → Allowed Domains** and save.
3. Update external scheduler targets and any Supabase cron configuration that still uses the old URL. See [Scheduler Jobs](/manual-installation/scheduler-jobs).
4. Verify wallet sign-in, admin access, and scheduled syncs on the new domain.

If [payments](/admin/integrations) are enabled, contact Kuest support before changing domains while payments are pending.

## Service subdomains

Optionally deploy a reverse proxy to keep supported Kuest service URLs under your domain. The proxy is separate from the frontend hosting configured above.

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Create the service proxies">
    <Tabs sync={false}>
      <Tab title="Deploy to Cloudflare" icon="cloud">
        Recommended. Enter your domain when Cloudflare asks for `DOMAIN`; the deployment creates the Worker, DNS records, and certificates.

        <a href="https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/?url=https://github.com/kuest/custom-domain">
          <img
            src="https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/button"
            alt="Deploy the Kuest custom-domain proxy to Cloudflare"
            noZoom
          />
          <span className="sr-only">Deploy the Kuest custom-domain proxy to Cloudflare</span>
        </a>

        Use a domain managed in your Cloudflare account, without `https://`, a path, or a port, such as `example.com` or `markets.example.com`. Each service hostname is created beneath that domain.

      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Manual setup" icon="settings">
        <AccordionGroup>
          <Accordion title="Cloudflare dashboard">
            Create a Worker with a `DOMAIN` variable, then attach these hostnames under **Settings > Domains & Routes > Add > Custom Domain**:

            ```text
            relayer.yourdomain.com
            clob.yourdomain.com
            data-api.yourdomain.com
            community.yourdomain.com
            create-market.yourdomain.com
            gamma-api.yourdomain.com
            price-reference.yourdomain.com
            geoblock.yourdomain.com
            sdk-download.yourdomain.com
            ws-live-data.yourdomain.com
            ws-subscriptions-clob.yourdomain.com
            ```

            Paste the [dashboard Worker](https://github.com/kuest/custom-domain/blob/main/examples/dashboard-worker.js) into the Cloudflare editor.
          </Accordion>

          <Accordion title="Other providers">
            Create the service subdomains you need as reverse proxies. Each hostname must forward its path, query, headers, body, and WebSocket upgrades to the matching `*.kuest.com` hostname listed in [`services.json`](https://github.com/kuest/custom-domain/blob/main/services.json).
          </Accordion>
        </AccordionGroup>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the proxy">
    Confirm that `https://clob.yourdomain.com` reaches the Kuest CLOB service and that the data and WebSocket hostnames you plan to use respond through your domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update environment variables">
    In Vercel or your hosting provider, replace `yourdomain.com` and save:

    ```bash
    CREATE_MARKET_URL=https://create-market.yourdomain.com
    DATA_URL=https://data-api.yourdomain.com
    CLOB_URL=https://clob.yourdomain.com
    RELAYER_URL=https://relayer.yourdomain.com
    GAMMA_URL=https://gamma-api.yourdomain.com
    COMMUNITY_URL=https://community.yourdomain.com
    PRICE_REFERENCE_URL=https://price-reference.yourdomain.com
    GEOBLOCK_URL=https://geoblock.yourdomain.com
    SDK_DOWNLOAD_URL=https://sdk-download.yourdomain.com
    WS_LIVE_DATA_URL=wss://ws-live-data.yourdomain.com
    WS_CLOB_URL=wss://ws-subscriptions-clob.yourdomain.com
    ```

    Redeploy the application after saving the variables.
  </Step>
</Steps>
